1. Show how you reason about domain rules. 
   - Explain why a meter reading, usage calculation, issued bill and adjustment are different concepts. Demonstrate how your design handles corrections without silently changing historical records.

2. Include a real failure investigation. 
   - Deliberately create a concurrency or retry bug, reproduce it, diagnose the root cause, implement a fix and write a short engineering postmortem. That tells an interviewer more about your engineering ability than another polished dashboard.

3. Document your decisions and trade-offs. 
   - Include a concise README explaining what you built, which assumptions you made, how you tested it and what you would change at greater scale. Kraken explicitly values documentation and the ability to work within complex systems.
